## S.I.T. Simple Inventory Tracker 

### Vision Statement 

SIT (Simple Inventor Tracker) is designed for small business owners to manage their inventory. The app is intended for businesses that require an easy to use inventory management solution without the complexity often associated with inventory trackers for much larger businesses.


The system will feature the ability to add, delete, and modify for products in their inventory. The app allows users to easily locate relevant product information by simply clicking on the desired product.


Products can be searched and sorted to allow the ability to find particular items faster. S.I.T will also be able to generate SKUs (Stock Keeping Unit) with either prefix or suffix to have unique product Ids while also being able to distinguish their products from those provided by the vendors.


While maintaining product information, S.I.T will also provide the feature to store vendor information that is associated with the store products. This will allow store clerks to quickly identify where products are sourced and allow access to the vendor information without needing to search external records.


S.I.T will maintain the integrity of the inventory by providing information of the current state of the inventory and will alert the inventory owners when products go below a desired stock amount. These alerts allow for users to identify products that are low-stock without needing to continuously manually monitor the inventory.


The system will also allow importing and exporting inventory data. This will allow users to easily backup their information as well as allow for easy data transfer between S.I.T and complementary apps.


The app will feature a scan SKU component that allows store clerks to scan the SKU of a product and immediately access product information and current stock quantity. After scanning, the clerk will be prompted to enter a stock amount to add or remove from the inventory. This will allow users to minimize the time used to manually do this process.
