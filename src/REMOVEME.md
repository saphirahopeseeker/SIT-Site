2. Product Demonstration

    A screen recording demonstrating major features
    Clear walkthrough of primary user flows
    Demonstration should match the functionality described above

You may use the Android emulator recorder or any other screen capture tool.
3. Development Postmortem

Provide a thoughtful reflection on your development process.

Discuss both strengths and weaknesses. This reflection will not negatively impact your grade.

Include discussion of the improvement area identified after your Iteration 2 retrospective:

    What changed?
    Was the improvement successful?
    What evidence supports that?

You may also choose several of the following topics:

    Overall architecture of your system
    What went well
    What went wrong
    What you would change if starting over
    Project size (classes, methods, components, etc.)
    Distribution of effort across layers
    Time spent on various tasks
    Design smells or strong design decisions
    Outstanding bugs or technical debt
    Unexpected technical challenges
    Use of techniques covered in the course (TDD, scrums, etc.)
    How the project evolved from Iteration 0 vision
    Lessons learned about teamwork and large projects

Professional Expectations

    Focus on systems and process, not individuals.
    Do not assign blame.
    Demonstrate ownership of your group outcome.
    Present the project as a cohesive team effort.
